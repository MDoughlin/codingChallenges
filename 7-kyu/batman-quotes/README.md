The Joker has mixed up their iconic quotes and also replaced one of the characters in their names, with a digit. They need help getting things back in order.</p><p>Implement the function which takes in an array of quotes, and a string comprised of letters and a single digit (e.g. <code>"Rob1n"</code>) where the number corresponds to their quote indexed in the passed in array.</p><p>Return the quote along with the character who said it:</p><pre><code>quotes = [  "I am vengeance. I am the night. I am Batman!",  "Holy haberdashery, Batman!",  "Let's put a smile on that faaaceee!"]hero = "Rob1n"Should output =&gt; "Robin: Holy haberdashery, Batman!"</code></pre><p>You are guaranteed that the number in the passed in string is placed somewhere after the first character of the string. The quotes either belong to <code>"Batman"</code>, <code>"Robin"</code>, or <code>"Joker"</code>.</p>
